Product Name Loan Against Life Insurance Policies 
Nature of Facility Term loan /OD 
Type (Secured / Unsecured) Secured  
Product Code OD against Life Insurance policy (132) 
Loan Against Life Insurance policy (559) 
Purpose Personal / Business 
Loan Amount  Minimum: Rs.2 lac 
Maximum: Rs.300.00 lac 
ROI ROI, as announced under monthly interest rates circular for loan 
products /as communicated from time to time. 
Processing Charges As per Latest SOC for loans & Advances 
Tenure For Overdraft:  
 
12 months/ Maturity date of the respective Life Insurance Policy 
whichever is earlier 
 
For Term Loan: 
 
Minimum loan period - 12 months 
Maximum Loan period - 60 months or Maturity date of the 
respective Life Insurance Policy whichever is earlier 
Margin 25% on surrender value confirmed by the insurer Company.  
10% in case of Staff Members of Dhanlaxmi Bank 
Repayment For Overdraft:  
Principal on demand. Interest to be serviced monthly. 
  
Surrender value should be adjusted to OD account on due da te of 
the policy 
 
For Term Loan:  
Bullet Repayment at the time of loan maturity

Only traditional life insurance policies (such as endowment, 
money-back, or whole life policies)  are generall y e ligible for 
assignment. Policies like unit-linked insurance plans (ULIPs) shall 
not be accepted.

Default Management Two-Month Non-Payment Clause 
For Overdraft 
If the customer fails to pay two consecutive monthly in terest, the 
branch must issue a notice by registered post acknowledgement 
due, to the borrower within seven days after the second missed 
payment stating that if within 30 days from the date of notice he 
fails to clear  the dues of the loan, the po licy would be surrendered 
without further reference to him.

Surrender of Policy 
 
If the customer does not regularize the account within 30 days of 
the notice, the branch must initiate the process to surrender the 
assigned poli cy. A letter is to be address ed to the Life Insurance 
company for the purpose. It should contain inter-alia, a certificate 
to the effect that the policy is being surrendered after due notice to 
the policy holder.  In such cases, the Life Insurance Company 
would pay the surrender value  to the Bank without serving any 
notice to the policy holder.  The p roceeds when received should be 
appropriated towards the dues under the advance.  All expenses 
should be recovered from the proceeds 
 
Note: 
 
The loan account m ust be closed using the proce eds from the 
surrendered policy value before the 90 th day from the first missed 
installment and balance amount, if any, shall be credited to 
customer’s SB account 
 
For Term Loan 
If the borrower fails to pay the policy premium, the branch must 
initiate the process to surrender the assigned policy after due notice 
to the borrower. Three overdue premium notices shall be served to 
the borrower with  total time of  90 days to pay the 
premium.ie.30days (Overdue premium Notice),30days ( Follow up 
Notice) and 30 days (Policy Surrender Notice ). Policy  shall be  
surrendered after 90 days from first notice, if premium is not paid 
by the borrower.  First Notice (Overdue Premium Notice) shall be 
served after 30 days from premium due date. (Format of Notices 
annexed). 
 
Surrender of Policy 
 
A letter to be addressed to the Insurance company for the purpose. 
It should contain inter-alia, a certificate to the effect that the policy 
is being surrendered after due notice to the policy holder.  In such 
cases, the Life Insurance Compan y would pay the surrender value 
to the Bank withou t serving any notice to the policy holder. The 
proceeds when received should be appropriated towards the dues 
under the advance.  All expenses should be recovered from the 
proceeds. 
 
In all cases, premium r eceipts should be regularly obtained and 
retained with the relative policies. The branch manager should 
ensure that premium is paid regularly.

Other conditions The following points should be noted while accepting life policies 
as security: 
 
➢ Normally, end owment policies, participating in profits 
should be preferred. 
➢ Policies issued under Married Woman’s property Act, 1874 
should not be accepted 
➢ Policies assigned to a Minor should not be accepted.  
➢ The policy must have a surrender value, and it should be 
assigned to the bank as primary security. 
➢ The policies where the premiums are paid for at least 3 years 
will be accepted. 
➢ Advance to third party is not permitted 
➢ The policy holder should give a letter addressed to the Life 
Insurance Company requesting the m to f urnish details of 
surrender value and bonus to the branch. It shall be 
collected along with loan application 
➢ Before the advance is sanction ed, the Policy should be 
assigned by the insured in favour of the Bank 
➢ The borrower should request Life Insuran ce Company by a 
separate letter to register the assignment in favour of the 
Bank and also declare that the policy has been assigned for 
valuable consideration. 
➢ Branch should obtain standing instructions from the 
borrower to pay the Premium on the policy as  and w hen 
they fall due.  
➢ In all cases, premium receipts should be regularly obtained 
and retained with the relative policies. The branch  manager 
should ensure that premium is paid 
➢ On maturity of the policy, an application should be made to 
the Company in the pr escribed format of respective 
insurance company forwarding the policy and a separate 
stamped receipt for maturity proceeds inclu ding accrued 
profits. The maturity dates should be diarized and claim 
papers should be forwarded in advance to the Life Insurance 
Company so that there is no delay in receipt of the policy 
amount on maturity 
➢ When the advance is repaid, policy should be re-assigned in 
the name of the policy holder. For re assignment, re 
assignment form along with notice of re assignment shall be 
submitted to the company in the prescribed format of 
respective insurance company. Both forms shall be signed 
by the Manager and  the Asst. Branch Manager jointly on 
behalf of the Bank
